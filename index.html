Short Hindi and Hinglish videos that question the beliefs we live by — about love, success, fear, family, society and happiness.

We make original short videos for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels. Each one takes a belief most of us never examine and turns it over, gently, with psychology and everyday Indian life.

Find us as the.manas.manthan on Instagram, and as The Manas Manthan on YouTube and Facebook.

About our publishing tool

The Manas Manthan uses a private tool to prepare its own videos and upload them to its own YouTube channel and social accounts, after the creator approves each video. It is not offered to the public and is used only by the channel's creator.

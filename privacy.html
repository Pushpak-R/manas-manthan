Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers the private publishing tool used by The Manas Manthan ("we") to upload and manage videos on its own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page. The tool is used only by the channel's creator; it has no other users and does not collect data from viewers.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access

  • Upload access to our own YouTube channel, to publish videos the creator has approved.
  • Read access to our own channel's videos, statistics and analytics (views, watch time, likes, comments, shares), to learn which videos work.
  • Public data from YouTube search and public comments on popular videos, read in aggregate to understand which topics audiences care about.
  • Comment access to post a comment as our own channel under our own videos.

How we use and store it

Access tokens are stored securely on our own server and used only by the tool. Video statistics and short comment excerpts are stored in our private database to improve future videos. We do not sell, rent or share any of this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept while they are useful for improving the channel. Public comment excerpts that were not used for a video idea are deleted automatically after 30 days. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party access; after that the tool can no longer access the channel, and the creator deletes the stored token.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on The Manas Manthan's YouTube channel "About" page.
